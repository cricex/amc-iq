## Renal Function and Dose Individualization

Carboplatin exposure is closely related to renal clearance. For regimens that target a planned area under the concentration-time curve (AUC), prescribers commonly estimate the dose using a Calvert-style relationship:

**Dose (mg) = target AUC x (estimated glomerular filtration rate or creatinine clearance + 25).**

Renal function should be assessed before treatment and monitored during therapy. When creatinine clearance is reduced, systemic exposure may increase, and the risk of myelosuppression may be greater. Protocols that use carboplatin often define creatinine clearance thresholds for enrollment, dose selection, or treatment delay.

## Practical Considerations

- Use the renal function equation specified by the protocol or treatment plan.
- Recalculate the estimate when serum creatinine changes materially.
- Consider repeat laboratory testing when a screening value is near a protocol threshold.
- Review concurrent nephrotoxic medications and hydration status when interpreting a borderline creatinine clearance value.
