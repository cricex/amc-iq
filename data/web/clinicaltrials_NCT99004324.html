A Phase II Study of an EGFR Exon 20 Insertion-Directed Therapy in Advanced NSCLC

NCT ID: NCT99004324 Recruiting

Condition: Metastatic NSCLC with EGFR exon 20 insertion

Phase: Phase 2

Eligibility

Sex: All   Age: 18 Years and older   Healthy volunteers: No

Key Inclusion Criteria

  • Histologically or cytologically confirmed metastatic non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC).
  • Documented EGFR exon 20 insertion by a validated local or central molecular assay.
  • ECOG performance status of 0 or 1.
  • Adequate organ function, including creatinine clearance (CrCl) >= 50 mL/min calculated by CKD-EPI within the screening window.

Key Exclusion Criteria

  • Prior therapy with an investigational EGFR exon 20 insertion-directed agent.
  • Untreated or unstable central nervous system metastases requiring escalating corticosteroids.
  • Clinically significant intercurrent illness that would interfere with protocol therapy or safety monitoring.
  • Ambiguous prior-platinum language: prior platinum-based chemotherapy, including carboplatin plus pemetrexed, may exclude a participant unless the investigator confirms eligibility under the current amendment.

Arms and Interventions

Experimental: Synthetic EGFR exon 20 insertion-directed therapy administered in 28-day cycles.
