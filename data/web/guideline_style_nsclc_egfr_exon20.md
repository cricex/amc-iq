# Synthetic Guideline-Style Summary: EGFR Exon 20 Insertion Metastatic NSCLC

This original synthetic summary is written in a guideline-like style for this proof-of-concept scenario. It does not reproduce NCCN, ASCO, ESMO, or other copyrighted guideline text and is not clinical decision support.

## Scope

Patients with metastatic non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) and an EGFR exon 20 insertion should have treatment planning guided by disease burden, prior systemic therapy, performance status, comorbidities, and trial availability.

## Management Principles

- Confirm the EGFR exon 20 insertion using a validated molecular assay and document the report in the trial-screening packet.
- For patients who have received platinum-based therapy, consider an EGFR exon 20-directed agent, clinical trial enrollment, or other systemic options according to local formulary and clinician judgment.
- Assess eligibility constraints that commonly affect trial enrollment, including ECOG performance status, treated or stable brain metastases, hematologic function, hepatic function, and renal function.
- When renal function is borderline for a protocol threshold, repeat creatinine and calculated clearance using the protocol-specified method before final eligibility determination.
- Discuss clinical trial participation early, especially when a study is biomarker-directed and available at the treating site.

## Trial Screening Notes

For a patient with prior carboplatin plus pemetrexed, review the protocol language carefully. Some studies require prior platinum exposure, while others exclude it or allow it only within a specified line-of-therapy amendment.
