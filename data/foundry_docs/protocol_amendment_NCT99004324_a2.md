# Amendment 2 Clarification

**Synthetic data. No PHI. Not clinical decision support.**

This Amendment 2 memo applies to **NCT99004324**, "A Phase II Study of an EGFR Exon 20 Insertion-Directed Therapy in Advanced NSCLC," at **AMC Thoracic Oncology Clinic (SITE-01)**. It clarifies two recurrent screening questions: interpretation of prior platinum-based chemotherapy and handling of borderline renal function.

## Prior Platinum-Based Chemotherapy

The original exclusion language referring to "prior platinum-based chemotherapy for metastatic NSCLC" is not intended to exclude a participant whose only platinum exposure was a standard **first-line platinum doublet** before consideration of this study. Prior platinum in the **first line** does **not** by itself exclude a candidate when the participant otherwise meets molecular, histologic, performance status, and organ function requirements.

Screening staff must document the regimen name, line of therapy, best response, and approximate treatment interval in the screening note. Because the protocol language remains broad, **PI confirmation is required** before eligibility is finalized for any participant with prior platinum exposure. Confirmation may be recorded as an addendum, screening checklist attestation, or email retained in the study binder.

## Renal Function

The renal threshold remains unchanged: **CrCl >= 50 mL/min (CKD-EPI)**. Creatinine clearance should be calculated from the most recent screening chemistry panel. When the value is borderline, discordant with the patient's clinical status, or below threshold by a small margin, the coordinator may request a repeat laboratory assessment after confirming timing with the treating oncologist and PI.

Borderline values do not create automatic eligibility. A value below 50 mL/min should be treated as not meeting the renal criterion unless repeat testing meets threshold and Dr. Priya Anand, **PI-01**, confirms the overall interpretation.
