## Synopsis

Trial **NCT99004324**, "A Phase II Study of an EGFR Exon 20 Insertion-Directed Therapy in Advanced NSCLC," is a recruiting investigator-led study at **AMC Thoracic Oncology Clinic (SITE-01)**. The study evaluates an EGFR exon 20 insertion-directed therapy for adults with advanced or metastatic non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC), adenocarcinoma or other NSCLC histology, whose tumors harbor a documented EGFR exon 20 insertion.

The Principal Investigator is **PI-01, Dr. Priya Anand**. Screening activities are coordinated through **COORD-01, Dana Whitfield**, with referral support from **NAV-01, Morgan Lee** when needed.

## Objectives

The primary objective is to estimate antitumor activity as measured by investigator-assessed response in participants with metastatic NSCLC and EGFR exon 20 insertion. Secondary objectives include describing durability of response, progression-free interval, overall tolerability, and the feasibility of molecularly directed trial enrollment in routine thoracic oncology practice.

## Eligibility Criteria

### Key Inclusion Criteria

Participants must have histologically confirmed metastatic NSCLC, including adenocarcinoma, with stage IV or otherwise advanced disease not appropriate for curative-intent local therapy. A documented **EGFR exon 20 insertion** must be present on a validated tumor or plasma molecular assay. ECOG performance status must be **0-1** at screening. Participants must have measurable or evaluable disease, adequate marrow and hepatic function, and be clinically stable enough to complete study visits at SITE-01.

Renal function must meet the study threshold: **CrCl >= 50 mL/min (CKD-EPI)** using the most recent available chemistry data during screening unless repeat testing is requested by the investigator.

### Key Exclusion Criteria

Participants are excluded for uncontrolled intercurrent illness, untreated or clinically unstable central nervous system disease, active infection requiring systemic therapy, or another malignancy expected to interfere with protocol assessments. Participants may not have received prior therapy directed specifically at EGFR exon 20 insertion using the investigational class under study.

The protocol text also states: "Subjects with prior platinum-based chemotherapy for metastatic NSCLC are excluded unless otherwise approved by the sponsor-investigator." This criterion is intentionally interpreted with caution because it does not specify line of therapy or whether adjuvant, first-line metastatic, or later-line platinum exposure is meant. Screening staff should consult current amendments and escalate unresolved cases to Dr. Priya Anand before marking a participant ineligible.
