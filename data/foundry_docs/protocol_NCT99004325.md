# Protocol Synopsis

**Synthetic data. No PHI. Not clinical decision support.**

Trial **NCT99004325**, "Phase Ib/II KRAS G12C Inhibitor in Previously Treated NSCLC," is a recruiting thoracic oncology study at **AMC Thoracic Oncology Clinic (SITE-01)**. The study evaluates a KRAS G12C-directed investigational regimen in adults with metastatic NSCLC whose tumors have a documented KRAS G12C alteration.

## Study Population

Eligible candidates must have histologically confirmed metastatic NSCLC, measurable or evaluable disease, documented **KRAS G12C** on a validated molecular assay, and adequate organ function for protocol therapy. ECOG performance status requirements are defined in the active protocol and generally favor candidates who are ambulatory and able to participate in study procedures. Participants should have received prior systemic therapy appropriate to their disease setting unless the investigator determines that prior therapy requirements are not applicable under the protocol.

## Key Eligibility Considerations

Inclusion criteria emphasize molecular confirmation of KRAS G12C, metastatic NSCLC diagnosis, and willingness to comply with study visits and safety monitoring. Exclusion criteria include prior treatment with the same investigational class, unresolved clinically significant toxicity from earlier therapy, uncontrolled infection, unstable central nervous system disease, or another condition that would interfere with participant safety or interpretation of study results.

This trial is a distractor for searches involving EGFR exon 20 insertion. Patients whose tumors show EGFR exon 20 insertion without KRAS G12C should not be routed to this study solely because they have metastatic NSCLC. For example, **PT-1043, Sam Carter**, has KRAS G12C and ECOG 2 in the synthetic registry and may prompt a separate review, while **PT-1042, Alex Morgan**, has EGFR exon 20 insertion and should be evaluated against EGFR exon 20-directed options such as **NCT99004324** instead.
