# Site Eligibility Escalation Rules

**Synthetic data. No PHI. Not clinical decision support.**

Eligibility questions at **AMC Thoracic Oncology Clinic (SITE-01)** should be escalated when a coordinator cannot make a clear determination from the active protocol, approved amendments, and source documents. The goal is to avoid both inappropriate exclusion of potential participants and inappropriate enrollment of candidates who do not meet protocol requirements.

Escalate to the Principal Investigator, **PI-01, Dr. Priya Anand**, when any of the following occur: protocol text contains ambiguous exclusions, the candidate has prior therapy that may be interpreted differently by line of therapy, the biomarker report is incomplete or discordant, ECOG status is not documented within the expected screening window, or organ function is close to a hard threshold.

Renal criteria require particular care. When a trial states a threshold such as **CrCl >= 50 mL/min (CKD-EPI)**, the coordinator should record the exact value, calculation method, date, and laboratory source. If the value is below threshold or borderline, the coordinator should not override the criterion. Instead, notify the treating oncologist and PI, ask whether a repeat chemistry panel is clinically appropriate, and document the decision.

For borderline renal values, repeat testing should occur within the protocol-allowed screening window whenever possible. A repeat value that meets threshold may support continued screening, but PI confirmation is still required if the earlier value raised concern. A repeat value below threshold should be treated as failing the renal criterion unless the protocol explicitly permits an exception.

All escalation decisions should be retained in the screening file, including who reviewed the issue, the evidence considered, and the final eligibility interpretation.
