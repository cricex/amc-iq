# Clinical Research Intake SOP

**Synthetic data. No PHI. Not clinical decision support.**

This SOP describes the standard intake workflow for thoracic oncology clinical trial referrals at **AMC Thoracic Oncology Clinic (SITE-01)**. It applies to provider referrals, molecular tumor board recommendations, registry-identified candidates, and navigator-generated referrals.

The intake process begins when a candidate is entered into the thoracic research queue. The coordinator, typically **COORD-01, Dana Whitfield**, confirms the patient identifier, diagnosis, stage, current treatment status, treating oncologist, and candidate trial. The coordinator then performs a preliminary document review using available clinic notes, pathology reports, molecular testing, medication history, and recent laboratory values.

For each candidate, the screening worksheet should separate objective findings from interpretation. Examples of objective findings include ECOG performance status, biomarker result, prior regimen and line of therapy, creatinine clearance method, and date of laboratory draw. Interpretive items, such as whether a broad exclusion applies, should be flagged rather than resolved independently when the protocol is unclear.

The coordinator must verify that the relevant protocol version and amendments are being used. If an amendment clarifies eligibility, the screening packet should cite both the original protocol section and amendment language. When required data are missing or stale, the coordinator requests updates through the treating team rather than assuming eligibility.

Final eligibility is not assigned during intake. Intake produces a concise recommendation: likely eligible, unlikely eligible, or needs review. Cases involving borderline renal function, ambiguous prior therapy exclusions, or discordant biomarker documentation should be escalated to the Principal Investigator before consent or enrollment steps proceed.
