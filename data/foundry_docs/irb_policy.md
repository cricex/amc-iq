# Institutional IRB Review Policy

**Synthetic data. No PHI. Not clinical decision support.**

The AMC IQ Institutional Review Board (IRB) provides ethical and regulatory oversight for human-subjects research conducted through AMC clinical programs, including oncology trials at **AMC Thoracic Oncology Clinic (SITE-01)**. All study activity must be reviewed and approved, or determined exempt when applicable, before any research-specific procedures occur.

For interventional oncology trials, the IRB submission package should include the protocol, investigator brochure or equivalent risk summary, consent form, recruitment materials, financial disclosure forms, and site-specific operational plan. Amendments must be submitted when eligibility language, study procedures, safety monitoring, or participant-facing materials change. Staff may not implement a substantive amendment until IRB approval is issued, except when immediate action is needed to protect participant safety.

The IRB expects protocol deviations and potential noncompliance to be reported according to institutional timelines. A deviation that affects eligibility, consent, participant rights, safety, or data integrity must be escalated to the Principal Investigator and research compliance office promptly. Administrative corrections that do not affect participants may be tracked in the study file.

Research personnel must complete required training before participating in screening, consenting, data abstraction, or study-drug accountability. Delegation logs must reflect the actual duties performed by each team member. For clinical trial candidates, the distinction between clinical care and research activity must be clear in the record.

This policy is general and does not replace protocol-specific eligibility criteria. When a trial document and institutional policy appear to conflict, the Principal Investigator should obtain guidance from the IRB or research compliance office before proceeding.
