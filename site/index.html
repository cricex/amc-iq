Proof of concept / synthetic data only

Connected Microsoft IQ architecture. Research AMC proof of concept. Human review stays in control.

AMC IQ is a proof of concept for a Microsoft IQ intelligence layer in a research academic medical center. The primary story is a connected/live architecture that brings institutional knowledge, structured clinical data, care-team workflow, and external evidence into one cited assessment. A deterministic local fallback remains available for reproducible walkthroughs and repeatable testing.

The scenario

A research-AMC trial-readiness question that crosses data boundaries.

"Patient with metastatic NSCLC, EGFR exon 20 insertion, prior platinum therapy, ECOG 1, CrCl 48. The open trial mentions renal thresholds and prior-therapy exclusions. Is the patient eligible, and what should the care team do next?"

A useful answer needs more than search. The proof of concept reconciles protocol language, patient facts, institutional policy, current workflow, and public evidence while surfacing uncertainty instead of hiding it.

How the proof of concept works

Connected retrieval. Explicit uncertainty. Human review.

  1. 01
    Interpret the question

    Resolve the patient, trial, requested decision, and missing facts.

  2. 02
    Retrieve in parallel

    Dispatch live or deterministic Foundry, Fabric, Work, and Web context requests concurrently.

  3. 03
    Match evidence to criteria

    Separate met criteria from blockers, uncertainty, and required follow-up.

  4. 04
    Draft a governed next action

    Return citations, an owner, and a task that remains drafted and unsubmitted.

Evidence you can inspect

01 / Trace

Citations and assessment steps stay visible.

Findings link back to their source material, while the assessment trace shows the specialist queries, deepening, reconciliation, and governed composition steps.

  • Source-level evidence for every material finding
  • Visible retrieval and specialist activity
  • Human-readable impact and unresolved issues

02 / Govern

The output ends in review, not automation.

The final assessment distinguishes potential match from confirmed eligibility, makes open issues prominent, and assigns a next action without submitting a task or sending a message.

Designed to be honest about the proof-of-concept boundary

01

Connected architecture first

The primary story is a connected Microsoft IQ architecture for a research AMC, with live Foundry, Fabric, Work, and external evidence integrations where access exists.

02

Deterministic local fallback

The full local walkthrough can run against deterministic synthetic data without an Azure account, authentication, or live service access so the experience stays reproducible.

03

Human review, no clinical claim

Tasks remain drafted, not submitted, and the cohort, protocols, identities, and assessments are fictional. AMC IQ is not validated for patient care, trial enrollment, or clinical decision support.
